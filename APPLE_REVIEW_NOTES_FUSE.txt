FUSE Iraq - App Review Notes

Do not store or paste live passwords into this repository.

Apple App Store Connect has a Review Information section for username and password.
Put reviewer credentials there only — never in git.

Reviewer login
- Open the app or https://www.fuseiraq.com/login
- Sign in with the App Store Connect review account provided in Review Information
- Customer access is enough for the public order flow
- Restaurant / driver / admin dashboards are role-gated and must use the matching review account if those screens are tested

Suggested review steps
1. Open the app home page.
2. Add an item from Home or Restaurants (Fayrouz).
3. Open Cart, change quantity, then sign in if asked.
4. Submit an order with a valid Iraqi phone (07XXXXXXXXX) and a full address.
5. Open Order status / طلباتي to see the created order.
6. Open Profile and sign out.
