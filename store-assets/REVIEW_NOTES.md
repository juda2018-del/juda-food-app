# App Review notes

Do not commit reviewer passwords.

In App Store Connect / Play Console, add a dedicated review account under the official review-credentials fields.

What the reviewer can do after login:
- Browse restaurants and menus
- Add catalog items to the cart
- Place a cash-on-delivery order
- Track order status
- Request account deletion from https://www.fuseiraq.com/data-deletion
- Contact support at https://www.fuseiraq.com/support

Role notes:
- A customer review account is enough for the public app.
- Restaurant and driver screens require matching role accounts provided only through the store review form.

Suggested path:
Home or Restaurants → Fayrouz → add item → Cart → sign in if asked → confirm order → Order status.
